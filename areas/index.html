Fort Myers

Fort Myers runs from the historic River District and older homes along McGregor Boulevard to Iona, Gateway, Whiskey Creek, San Carlos Park and newer communities off Daniels Parkway. Older homes may have cast iron drains and aging supply lines; condos and townhomes see leaks from units above; and neighborhoods along the Caloosahatchee River face surge and river flooding during storms. Most common: AC leak water damage, condo water damage and cast iron pipe water damage. Full guide: water damage restoration in Fort Myers.

Cape Coral

Mostly single-family slab homes, many built between 1970 and 1999, laced with canals. City water, sewer and a separate irrigation system serve most of the Cape, while parts of the north still run on wells and septic. Slab leaks, original cast iron drains in older Cape neighborhoods, canal surge and leaks in seasonal homes are the most common calls. Full guide: water damage restoration in Cape Coral.

Lehigh Acres

A large, younger community of concrete block homes, many built in the 2000s and later. Many homes use private wells and septic tanks, heavy summer rain can flood streets and low lots, and air conditioners run hard most of the year. Full guide: water damage help in Lehigh Acres.

North Fort Myers

North of the Caloosahatchee, North Fort Myers mixes riverfront homes, golf communities and many manufactured-home communities. Soft floors from water heater and supply line leaks, water trapped in the belly wrap, roof leaks and river flooding are the most common problems. Full guide: water damage restoration in North Fort Myers. See also mobile home water damage.

Fort Myers Beach

Fort Myers Beach on Estero Island sits right on the Gulf, and many homes and businesses there have been rebuilt, often elevated. Storm surge, condo and vacation rental leaks and sewage problems after storms are the main concerns. Full guide: water damage help on Fort Myers Beach.

Sanibel and Captiva

Many seasonal owners, condos and elevated homes. Hurricane surge is the biggest risk, followed by leaks that go unnoticed while owners are away. See water damage in vacant homes and flood and storm cleanup.

Pine Island and Matlacha

Pine Island, Bokeelia, St. James City and Matlacha are low-lying and surrounded by water, so surge is a real threat. Many manufactured homes and seasonal residents mean floor and belly-wrap damage and leaks found after time away.

Estero

Gated communities, condos, coach homes and seasonal residents. The most common water damage is a leak from the unit above, an AC closet overflow, or a home found wet and musty when the owner returns. Full guide: water damage restoration in Estero.

Bonita Springs

Condos along the coast and neighborhoods near the Imperial River. River and rain flooding, condo leaks and seasonal-home damage are the main concerns. Full guide: water damage restoration in Bonita Springs.

San Carlos Park, Gateway and Iona in Lee County

These communities carry Fort Myers addresses but sit in unincorporated Lee County. San Carlos Park has single-family homes from several decades; Gateway is a planned community with mostly newer homes; Iona, near the Gulf and the Caloosahatchee, has canal-front and older homes that face storm surge.

What to Do First Anywhere in Lee County

  • Shut off the water at the fixture or main shut-off. On a well, also turn off the pump.
  • Cut power at the breaker to rooms where water reaches outlets or appliances.
  • Photograph everything before cleanup.
  • Start drying the same day.

What You Can Expect Anywhere in Lee County

  • A clear explanation of the water category and what that means for what stays and what goes.

Questions About Water Damage Help Across Lee County

Do you cover my area of Lee County?

Fort Myers, Cape Coral, Lehigh Acres, North Fort Myers, Fort Myers Beach, Sanibel, Captiva, Pine Island, Estero, Bonita Springs, San Carlos Park, Gateway and Iona are covered. If you're nearby and not sure, call and ask.

Is water damage help available at night and on weekends?

Yes. Call 239-356-3708. It's answered 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays.

Do you help after hurricanes on the islands?

Yes, once officials allow access. After a major storm, call early and photograph everything while you wait.

Is water damage different on the coast than inland?

The drying process is the same, but the causes differ: surge and condos on the coast, wells, septic and street flooding inland, cast iron and slab leaks in older neighborhoods.

How soon can someone come out?

It depends on where you are, the time of day and how many calls are coming in. After a hurricane or major flood, waits across Lee County can be longer, so call early and start the first-hour steps while you wait.

Which parts of Lee County have the highest flood risk?

Barrier islands such as Fort Myers Beach, Sanibel, Captiva and Pine Island face the most storm surge, followed by canal-front homes in Cape Coral, Iona and areas along the Caloosahatchee and Imperial rivers.

My address says Fort Myers. Which building department handles my repairs?

It depends on whether the home is inside the City of Fort Myers limits or in unincorporated Lee County. Check your property tax bill or the Lee County Property Appraiser's record.

Do you serve Collier or Charlotte County?

This service focuses on Lee County. If you're just outside it, call and ask.

What should I do while I wait for help?

Shut off the water, cut power to wet rooms, photograph everything, move belongings off wet floors and keep the AC running to pull moisture out of the air if the water is clean.
