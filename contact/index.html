Before You Call: If Water Is Still Coming Into Your Fort Myers Home

  • Turn off power at the breaker to wet rooms, if you can reach the panel without standing in water.
  • Shut off the water at the fixture or the main. On a private well, also turn off the pump.
  • Photograph everything before you move anything.

What to Have Ready When You Call About Fort Myers Water Damage

  • Your address, ZIP code and the best number to reach you
  • What happened and when you found it
  • Where the water came from, if you know
  • Which rooms are wet and roughly how big the area is
  • Whether the water is clean, from an appliance, or from a toilet, drain or flood
  • Whether the water and power are off
  • Condo or gated community: building management contact and gate instructions
  • Your insurance company, if you plan to file a claim

Calling About a Lee County Home You're Not At

Many calls come from seasonal owners up north, landlords, property managers and family members. If you're not at the home:

  • Name the person who can open the door: a home watch service, neighbor, property manager or building management.
  • Have the gate code, lockbox details or front desk contact ready.
  • Ask whoever is there to shut off the water and send you photos before anything is moved.
  • Give the best number to reach you for approvals, since you'll get the written scope before work beyond emergency water removal.

Questions About Contacting Fort Myers Water Damage Restoration Pros

Can I get a price over the phone?

Not an exact one. The price depends on how far water spread inside walls and under flooring, which has to be measured on site. For a rough idea, see the water damage pricing guide.

Do you cover my city?

Fort Myers, Cape Coral, Lehigh Acres, North Fort Myers, Fort Myers Beach, Estero and Bonita Springs are covered. If you're nearby and not sure, call and ask.

Should I call my insurer first?

Stop the water and make it safe first, then report the loss to your insurer as soon as you can. See how insurance handles water damage in Florida.

Can I call for a home I'm not at right now?

Yes. Owners, landlords, property managers and family members call for homes all the time. Have someone ready to open the door and the gate or building access details on hand.

I rent my home. Should I call you or my landlord?

Tell your landlord or property manager right away, because Florida law generally makes the landlord responsible for keeping the plumbing in working order and repairs are usually theirs to arrange. Your renters policy covers your belongings.
