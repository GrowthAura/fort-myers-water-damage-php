Key facts about slab leaks in Lee County

  • Most Fort Myers, Cape Coral and Lehigh Acres homes are slab-on-grade, with supply lines running in or under the concrete.
  • Hot water line leaks are the ones people notice most, because they warm the floor.
  • Vinyl plank and laminate trap water against the slab and usually have to be lifted.
  • Insurance often depends on whether the leak was sudden and whether your policy has tear-out coverage.

Think You Have a Slab Leak in Your Fort Myers Home? Do This First

  • Check the water meter. Turn off every faucet, appliance and the irrigation. If the meter still moves, water is escaping somewhere.
  • On a private well, common in parts of Lehigh Acres, listen for the pump kicking on when no water is running.
  • Shut off the main water if the floor is wet or the meter is spinning fast.
  • Turn off the water heater at its breaker, or set a gas heater to pilot, if you suspect the hot water line.
  • Photograph wet areas, warm spots, stained grout and damp baseboards.
  • Call a plumber for leak detection, then start drying the same day.

Signs of a Slab Leak in a Fort Myers Home

  • A warm or hot spot on tile or vinyl floors, usually from a hot water line
  • The sound of running water when nothing is on
  • A water meter that moves, or a well pump that runs, with every fixture off
  • A sudden jump in your water bill, or an electric bill that climbs because the water heater never stops
  • Damp or swollen baseboards with no visible leak above
  • Dark or wet grout lines, loose or cracked tile, or vinyl plank that lifts
  • A musty smell or mold along the bottom of walls
  • Low water pressure
  • Water pooling near the foundation outside when it hasn't rained

Why Slab Leaks Happen in Fort Myers and Lee County Homes

Slab-on-grade construction is the norm across Lee County. Supply lines run below or inside the concrete, so when one fails, the water has nowhere to go but sideways under your floors. Common causes:

  • Corrosion of older copper lines, including the thin-wall copper used under many older slabs, which can develop pinholes over time
  • Abrasion where a pipe rubs against concrete, gravel or another pipe as it expands and contracts with hot water
  • Ground movement and settling in sandy soil
  • High water pressure that stresses every joint
  • Damage or poor installation during original construction

Not every leak under the slab is a supply line. A cracked drain line carries wastewater instead; see cast iron pipe water damage. A leak you can see above the floor is a different job; see burst pipe water damage.

When Can New Flooring Go Down on a Fort Myers Slab?

Not when the surface feels dry. Concrete keeps releasing water for days or weeks, and Fort Myers humidity slows it down. Vinyl plank, laminate and wood laid on a slab that is still wet can cup, lift or grow mold underneath. Before new flooring goes down, flooring makers usually require a slab moisture test, such as in-slab relative humidity probes under ASTM F2170, and your installer should check the result against the flooring's limits.

Is Slab Leak Water in a Fort Myers Home Clean?

A leaking supply line carries clean water, Category 1, when caught early. Water that has sat under flooring for days or weeks can become Category 2, and long-standing moisture under vinyl plank or carpet often brings mold. The longer a slab leak runs, the more material comes out.

Does Insurance Cover a Slab Leak for Fort Myers Homeowners?

This is general information, not advice about your policy.

  • The resulting water damage to floors, walls and cabinets is often covered when the leak was sudden.
  • The pipe repair itself may not be covered.
  • Tear-out coverage, in some policies, pays to break out the slab or open walls to reach the leak.
  • Long-running leaks may be excluded as continuous or repeated seepage.
  • Some Florida policies cap plumbing-leak damage through a limited water damage endorsement, often at $10,000 per loss.

Keep the plumber's leak detection report, photos and your water bills, which can show when the leak started. Read how insurance handles water damage in Florida.

How to Lower the Risk of Another Slab Leak in Your Fort Myers Home

  • Have your water pressure checked. Florida's plumbing code calls for a pressure-reducing valve when static pressure is above 80 psi.
  • Watch your water bill, and check the meter if it jumps.
  • Install a leak-detecting shut-off valve that closes the main when it senses continuous flow.
  • Ask about rerouting or repiping if you've had more than one slab leak.

Questions About Slab Leak Water Damage in Fort Myers

How do I know if I have a slab leak?

Turn off every fixture and check the water meter. If it moves, look for warm spots on the floor, damp baseboards or the sound of running water, and call a plumber for leak detection.

How do I check for a slab leak if my home is on a well?

Turn off every fixture and listen at the pressure tank. If the pump kicks on with no water running, water is escaping somewhere. Call a plumber to test the lines.

Is a slab leak an emergency?

Yes, if water is surfacing on the floor or the meter is spinning fast. Shut off the main water and start drying. A slow leak still needs prompt attention before it damages flooring and grows mold.

Can tile be saved after a slab leak?

Often, if it's still firmly bonded. The grout lines and slab are dried in place. Loose, hollow or cracked tile is replaced.

How long does it take to dry a slab after a leak?

Longer than a surface leak. Concrete releases water slowly, so slab drying often takes more than the 3 to 5 days typical for drywall and carpet.

Should I reroute or repair the leaking line?

That's the plumber's call. One leak in an otherwise sound line is often spot-repaired; repeat leaks or old copper often lead to rerouting or repiping.

Can a slab leak damage my foundation?

Most slab leaks that are found and fixed promptly don't. A leak that runs for a long time can wash out sandy soil under the slab in some cases, so if new cracks or settling appear, have the plumber and a structural engineer look.

Can a slab leak cause mold?

Yes. Moisture trapped under vinyl plank, carpet or baseboards for days or weeks can grow mold in Florida humidity. Drying should start as soon as the line is repaired.

Can I stay in my house while the slab is dried?

Usually yes for a clean-water leak, with equipment running in the affected rooms. Large tear-outs or kitchen damage may make some rooms unusable for a while.
