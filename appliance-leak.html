Key facts about appliance leaks in Fort Myers

  • Washer hoses, dishwasher connections and ice maker lines cause most appliance leaks.
  • Ice maker and dishwasher leaks are often slow and can run for weeks before a soft cabinet floor or lifting plank gives them away.
  • Particle board cabinet floors and toe kicks swell and don't recover.
  • In many Lee County homes the washer sits in the garage, so an overflow spreads across the slab and under the door into the house.
  • In condos, stacked laundry closets mean a washer overflow can reach the ceiling below almost right away.

Appliance Leaking in Your Fort Myers Home? Do This First

  • Turn the appliance off. Unplug it only if you can reach the plug without standing in water; otherwise switch off its breaker.
  • Close its water valve. Washers have hot and cold valves on the wall box. Dishwashers usually shut off under the kitchen sink. Ice maker lines often have a small valve under the sink or behind the fridge.
  • Can't find the valve? Shut off the main water. On a well, switch off the pump too.
  • Water coming out of the pipe behind the washer? That's the laundry drain backing up. Stop the washer and don't run it again until the drain is cleared. If toilets or tubs are gurgling or backing up too, the main drain may be blocked. See sewage backup cleanup.
  • Soak up standing water and move rugs, boxes and belongings out of it.
  • Photograph the appliance, the failed hose or line, its model label and the floor.
  • In a condo, tell the unit below and building management right away.

Where Laundry Rooms Sit in Lee County Homes, and Where the Water Goes

  • Garage: common in Fort Myers, Cape Coral and Lehigh Acres single-family homes. Water spreads across the slab, wicks into the bottom of the garage drywall and runs under the door into the house.
  • Interior laundry room: water slips under baseboards into the hallway and nearby bedrooms, often under vinyl plank.
  • Condo laundry closet: with stacked units, an overflow can come through the ceiling below. See condo water damage.
  • Second-floor laundry: in two-story homes and townhomes, an overflow soaks the subfloor and the ceiling underneath. See ceiling leak water damage.
  • Manufactured homes: a washer leak soaks the particle board decking and the floor system below. See mobile home water damage.

Why Appliance Leaks Do More Damage Than They Look in Fort Myers

  • Water goes under things. It runs under toe kicks, beneath cabinet floors and under vinyl plank or laminate, where it's trapped against the slab.
  • Leaks are often slow. An ice maker line or dishwasher connection can drip for weeks before a soft cabinet floor, lifting plank or musty smell gives it away.
  • Particle board swells. Most cabinet bases are particle board, and once it swells it doesn't go back. See water damaged kitchen cabinets.
  • Florida humidity slows drying. Water trapped under cabinets and flooring dries slowly here on its own, which gives mold time to grow.

Appliance Leaks in Fort Myers Condos

Under Florida Statute 718.111(11), the association's policy generally leaves appliances, water heaters, water filters, built-in cabinets, countertops and floor, wall and ceiling coverings inside a unit to the owner's HO-6 policy. If a washer hose or ice maker line in one unit damages another, who pays often depends on the cause and whether the appliance was kept up, and your declaration can change parts of the default. Report it to building management and your insurer the same day. See condo water damage.

How to Prevent the Next Appliance Leak in Your Fort Myers Home

  • Replace rubber washer hoses with braided stainless hoses, and replace hoses about every five years.
  • Leave a few inches behind the washer so the hoses don't kink.
  • Turn off the washer valves when you leave town.
  • Use a washer drain pan in condos, second-floor laundry rooms and over finished floors.
  • Check the ice maker line whenever the fridge is moved; braided lines hold up better than thin plastic tubing.
  • Put leak alarms or an automatic shut-off valve under sinks, behind the fridge and next to the washer.
  • Don't run the washer or dishwasher while nobody is home.
  • Seasonal homes: close the washer valves and the main before you leave. See water damage in vacant homes.

Does Insurance Cover Appliance Leak Damage in Fort Myers?

This is general information, not advice about your policy.

  • Sudden leaks, such as a burst washer hose, are often covered for the resulting damage.
  • The appliance or the failed hose itself usually isn't.
  • Slow leaks that ran for weeks may be excluded as continuous seepage.
  • Some Florida policies cap non-weather water losses, often at $10,000 per loss, through a limited water damage endorsement.
  • Citizens policyholders: Citizens offers its own water removal program for non-weather losses like appliance leaks, and its published materials say emergency water mitigation by your own company is limited to $3,000.

Questions About Appliance Leaks in Fort Myers

My washing machine overflowed. What should I do first?

Turn the washer off, close the hot and cold valves behind it and soak up the water. Photograph everything, then check the walls and floors in nearby rooms, the garage or the unit below.

Why is water coming out of the pipe behind my washer?

That pipe is the laundry drain, called a standpipe. When it overflows during the drain cycle, the line is usually clogged with lint or buildup. Stop the washer, and if other drains in the house are slow or backing up too, call a plumber before using more water.

Can a dishwasher leak damage the floor under the cabinets?

Yes. Dishwasher water runs under the cabinets and toe kicks and soaks particle board cabinet floors and the flooring beneath. Pull the toe kick and check with a moisture meter.

How do I know if my refrigerator is leaking under the floor?

Look for a puddle that keeps returning, lifting or soft vinyl plank, stained grout or a musty smell near the fridge. A cracked ice maker line can leak slowly for weeks.

Why is there water under my fridge when the ice maker line is fine?

A clogged defrost drain is a common cause: melt water from the freezer overflows the drain pan underneath. A cracked water filter housing or heavy condensation can also leave water there. Pull the fridge out and check behind and under it.

Can a water softener leak cause water damage?

Yes. In well homes in Lehigh Acres, north Cape Coral and North Fort Myers, softeners often sit in the garage or a utility room, and a brine tank overflow or loose fitting can soak the floor and the bottom of nearby drywall. Close its bypass valve and have it checked.

Is washing machine water dangerous?

Drain water from a washer is gray water, Category 2. It isn't sewage, but it carries detergent, soil and bacteria, so wet carpet pad and porous materials are usually removed and the area is cleaned before drying.

Can a refrigerator leak cause mold?

Yes. A slow ice maker or defrost drain leak can keep the floor and cabinets damp for weeks, which is enough for mold in Florida humidity. Pull the fridge out and check behind and under it.

Should I keep using the washer after it leaked?

No. Leave it off with the valves closed until the hose, drain or machine has been checked.

How long does it take to dry after an appliance leak?

Most appliance leaks dry in about 3 to 5 days. Leaks that soaked cabinets or ran under vinyl plank take longer.
