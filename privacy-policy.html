Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information Fort Myers Water Damage Restoration Pros ("we", "us", "our") collects when you visit fortmyerswaterdamagerestoration.us (the "Site") or call 239-356-3708, how that information is used, and the choices you have. By using the Site or calling us, you agree to the practices described here.

Information you give us

When you call, submit a form or contact us another way, you may share:

  • Your name, phone number and email address.
  • The address of the property with water damage.
  • A description of the problem, such as the source of the water, the rooms affected and when it started.
  • Photos or videos you choose to send.
  • Details about your insurance company or claim number, if you choose to share them.

You do not have to share insurance details to get help.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the Site, some information is collected automatically by our hosting provider, analytics tools and your browser, including:

  • Your IP address and general location (city or region level).
  • Browser type, device type and operating system.
  • Pages you view, the page that referred you and the time spent on each page.
  • Clicks on phone numbers and form submissions.

This information is used in aggregate to understand how the Site is used and to fix problems.

Cookies and similar technologies

The Site may use cookies and similar technologies for basic functions, to measure traffic and to understand which pages are helpful. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking cookies will not stop you from calling us or reading the Site.

Phone calls

Calls to 239-356-3708 may be handled by a call-answering or call-tracking service so that the line can be answered 24/7. Calls may be recorded for quality and training purposes. Where a call is recorded, you will be told at the start of the call.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your call or message and arrange help for your property.
  • Schedule a visit, send you updates and keep a record of the work requested.
  • Share job details with the restoration crew or service provider who will carry out the work.
  • Improve the Site and understand which pages answer people's questions.
  • Keep the Site secure and prevent misuse.
  • Meet legal, accounting and record-keeping obligations.

When we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With the restoration company, crew member or contractor who will assess or work on your property, so they can contact you and do the job.
  • With service providers who help us run the business, such as website hosting, call answering, scheduling and email tools. They may use the information only to provide those services to us.
  • With your insurance company or adjuster, only if you ask us to.
  • When required by law, a court order or to protect the rights, property or safety of you, us or others.
  • As part of a sale, merger or transfer of the business, in which case this policy continues to apply to the information transferred.

Text messages

If you give us your mobile number, we may text you about your service request, such as arrival times or updates. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop texts at any time. We do not send marketing texts without your consent, and mobile numbers are not shared with third parties for their own marketing.

How long we keep information

We keep job and contact records for as long as needed to provide the service, answer follow-up questions, support any insurance claim and meet legal and tax requirements. After that, records are deleted or anonymized.

How we protect information

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical measures to protect your information. No website, phone system or email is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security. Please do not send highly sensitive details, such as full bank or card numbers, through the Site's forms or by email.

Your choices

  • You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, unless we must keep it for legal reasons.
  • You can opt out of non-essential emails or texts at any time.
  • You can block cookies in your browser.

To make a request, call 239-356-3708.

Children's privacy

The Site is meant for adults arranging help for a property. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Links to other websites

The Site links to outside websites such as FEMA, the National Flood Insurance Program and Lee County government pages. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites. Please read their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. Continued use of the Site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
