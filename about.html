Water Damage Restoration in Lehigh Acres

ZIP codes 33936, 33971, 33972, 33973, 33974 and 33976. Many homes run on a private well and septic, so pressure tanks, softeners and septic backups join AC closet and water heater leaks; FGUA provides central water and sewer in some areas. See water damage restoration in Lehigh Acres.

Water Damage Restoration in North Fort Myers

ZIP codes 33903 and 33917. Manufactured-home and 55+ communities, Caloosahatchee and creek flooding, and wells and septic on larger lots such as Suncoast Estates. North Fort Myers is unincorporated, so Lee County handles permits. See water damage restoration in North Fort Myers.

Water Damage Restoration on Fort Myers Beach

ZIP code 33931. Storm surge, enclosures under elevated homes, and condo and vacation rental leaks on Estero Island. The Town of Fort Myers Beach handles permits on the island; San Carlos Island, across Matanzas Pass, is Lee County. See water damage restoration on Fort Myers Beach.

Water Damage Restoration in Estero

ZIP code 33928. Condo and coach home leaks, AC closet overflows, seasonal homes and Estero River flooding in gated communities across the village. The Village of Estero handles permits and the 50% rule. See water damage restoration in Estero.

Water Damage Restoration in Bonita Springs

ZIP codes 34134 and 34135. Imperial River flooding, coastal condos near Bonita Beach and gated communities with seasonal owners. The City of Bonita Springs handles permits, and Bonita Springs Utilities supplies water and sewer. See water damage restoration in Bonita Springs.

How We Work in Fort Myers Homes

  • Measured, not guessed. Wet areas are mapped with moisture meters and a thermal camera, and drying ends when readings say dry, not when a surface feels dry.
  • Plain explanations. You hear what is wet, what can be dried in place and what likely has to come out, and why.
  • In writing first. You get the scope in writing before work beyond emergency water removal.
  • Your claim stays yours. No one asks you to sign over insurance benefits; for Florida residential policies issued on or after January 1, 2023, post-loss assignment of benefits isn't allowed.
  • Your choice on repairs. Rebuilding is your decision, and you choose who does it.

Questions About Fort Myers Water Damage Restoration Pros

Do you fix the leak itself?

No. The source is fixed by a plumber, roofer or HVAC technician. The work here is getting the water out, drying the structure and documenting it.

Is the information on this site advice about my insurance policy?

No. It's general information about how Florida water damage claims usually work. Your policy, your insurer and, if you need one, a professional you hire are the places to get advice about your claim.
