## Verdict in one paragraph

The site is correct, legible and well-built — but it reads as a **competent template**, not a premium brand. One display weight (Archivo Black 400) does every heading on every page; one 0.93→0.70 dark scrim buries every hero photo under the same grey-teal wash; the 12,000–22,000px body is a single white column with no banding, no kickers and no visual crescendo; and gold + sand — the two colours that could make this palette feel Floridian-luxury — appear almost only as 3px rules. The single biggest luxury lever is letting the photography breathe: the Cape Coral canal, the Fort Myers Beach stilt house and the burst-pipe spray are genuinely beautiful images that currently look like murky stock.

## RANKED LUXURY UPGRADES (rank 1 = highest impact)

### 1 — Hero scrim buries every photo under the same dark wash (ALL 9 pages)
- **What is wrong now:** `.hero-scrim` is `linear-gradient(100deg, rgba(6,32,40,0.93) 0%, rgba(6,32,40,0.80) 46%, rgba(6,32,40,0.70) 100%)` — 70–93% opaque dark teal across the FULL hero width, on every page. Measured hero height 571–683px; the photo is visible only as a grey-teal ghost. Worst cases viewed: `cape-coral-d-hero.png` — a bright canal scene (palms, boats, blue water) renders as murky black water and grey palms; `fort-myers-beach-d-hero.png` — stilt house + Gulf + sand renders grey, and the bottom ~200px of the 571px hero is an empty dark sea band; `home-d-hero.png` — the living-room photo is a dark silhouette with a grey sofa; `cost-d-hero.png` — the worker is a near-black silhouette against a mouldy wall. The scrim is doing the job of contrast, but at the price of killing the only colour and place-feel on first paint.
- **Concrete upgrade:** directional scrim, not uniform: `linear-gradient(100deg, rgba(6,32,40,0.92) 0%, rgba(6,32,40,0.78) 38%, rgba(6,32,40,0.38) 68%, rgba(6,32,40,0.22) 100%)` + a bottom-only gradient `linear-gradient(0deg, rgba(6,32,40,0.55) 0%, transparent 32%)` for the areas line. Then per-hero tune: area pages (cape-coral, fort-myers-beach) drop the right-hand stop to 0.12 so the water/sky reads; service pages keep 0.30 on the right. Add `filter: saturate(1.12) contrast(1.04)` on `.hero-img`. Keep white H1 contrast ≥7:1 by keeping the left 40% dark — the text never sits on the light side (H1 max-width 960px already guarantees this).
- **Expected effect:** first paint stops looking like a dark template and starts looking like Florida — canal blue, Gulf turquoise and the burst-pipe spray become visible while text stays perfectly legible. This one change lifts all 9 heroes at once.
