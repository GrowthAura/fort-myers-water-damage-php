How Structural Drying Works in Fort Myers Homes
The steps below follow the IICRC S500, the industry standard for water damage restoration that defines water categories, drying classes and what "dry" means.
1. Map the moisture
Moisture meters and a thermal camera show exactly which walls, floors and cabinets are wet. Dry areas of the same house are measured too; they become the target the wet materials must reach.
2. Remove what can't dry
Wet carpet pad, soaked insulation, swollen particle board and anything touched by sewage come out. Standing water is extracted first through emergency water removal (/water-removal/).
3. Set the equipment
Low-grain refrigerant (LGR) dehumidifiers pull water vapor out of the air. Air movers blow across wet walls and floors so moisture evaporates faster. Desiccant dehumidifiers are used for dense materials like concrete and hardwood, and in tight spaces where refrigerant units struggle.
4. Open up hidden spaces
Baseboards may come off and small holes are drilled behind them so dry air reaches inside the wall cavity. Cabinet toe kicks come off so air can move under the cabinets. Wet vinyl plank or laminate may be lifted so the slab can breathe.
5. Measure every day
Moisture readings and indoor humidity are checked and logged as the job goes on, and equipment is moved as areas dry. The logs show what was wet, how wet, and when it reached dry.
6. Confirm it's dry
Drying is finished when wet materials match the dry readings of the same materials elsewhere in the house. Only then do drywall, baseboards and flooring go back.
Why Drying Takes Longer in Fort Myers Homes
• Humid outdoor air. Southwest Florida air holds a lot of moisture most of the year, so open windows slow drying down instead of speeding it up.
• Concrete slabs. Most Lee County homes are slab-on-grade. Concrete soaks up water and releases it slowly, and tile, vinyl plank and laminate trap it underneath.
• Concrete block walls. Many homes have block exterior walls with drywall over furring strips, which can hold moisture in the gap behind the drywall.
• Slow, hidden leaks. AC condensate overflows and supply-line drips often run for days before anyone notices, so more material is wet when drying starts.
• Seasonal homes. A leak in a closed-up home can soak a house for weeks. See water damage in vacant homes (/vacant-home/).
Drying Fort Myers Block Homes, Ceilings, Condos and Mobile Homes
Concrete block walls
Drywall on the outside walls of most Fort Myers homes sits on wood furring strips over block, often with foil-faced insulation board in the gap. That layer slows drying from the room side, so the cavity is opened at the bottom and dried directly, and the furring strips are read separately from the drywall.
Tile on a slab
Tile that is still bonded can usually stay, and the slab dries through the grout lines. Tiles that sound hollow or have popped loose are lifted so the concrete under them can release its water.
Ceilings under an attic air handler
When an attic AC unit overflows, wet blown insulation sits on the ceiling drywall and keeps it wet from above. The wet insulation is removed, and a sagging ceiling is taken down before it falls. Turn off power to that room and stay out from under a bulging ceiling. See ceiling leak water damage (/ceiling-leak/).
Condos
Water crosses into the unit below and into walls shared with neighbors. Drying has to cover every affected unit, and the association is kept in the loop so access to each unit is lined up. See condo water damage (/condo-leak/).
Mobile and manufactured homes
Water collects in the belly wrap under the floor and soaks the particle board subfloor. The belly is opened and dried from below, and soft subfloor is replaced. See mobile home water damage (/mobile-home/).
Why Box Fans and a Home Dehumidifier Aren't Enough in Fort Myers Humidity
They can help with a small surface spill. After a real leak they fall short because:
• They can't push air inside walls, under tile or behind cabinets.
• A household dehumidifier removes a small fraction of the water a commercial LGR unit pulls from the air each day.
• Without moisture readings, nobody knows when the wall or slab is actually dry, so drywall gets closed up wet.
• Moving damp air around in Florida humidity can spread moisture into dry rooms.

Does Wet Drywall Have to Be Cut Out in Fort Myers Homes?
Not always. Drywall wet with clean water and dried quickly can often stay. It is usually cut out when:
• The water was sewage, floodwater or another Category 3 source.
• The insulation behind it is soaked.
• It sat wet long enough to soften, swell, crumble or grow mold.
When drywall is removed, a straight flood cut is made above the highest wet point, often about 2 or 4 feet up the wall, so the repair is clean and the framing can dry.
How Slabs, Floors and Cabinets Are Dried in Fort Myers
• Concrete slabs dry slowly. Vinyl plank or laminate on top usually has to be lifted so the concrete can release moisture. Tile that is still bonded can often stay while the slab dries through the grout.
• Hardwood may be dried in place with drying mats if work starts early; cupped or buckled boards usually come out.
• Cabinets are dried by removing toe kicks and pushing dry air underneath. Swollen particle board bases are replaced.

Living With Drying Equipment in Your Fort Myers Home
Drying works only while the equipment runs, so a few days of noise and warm air are part of the job.
• Leave it running day and night. Turning it off lets humidity climb back and adds days to the job.
• Keep the AC on. Dehumidifiers release warm, dry air, so rooms feel warmer than usual. The AC keeps the house comfortable and helps hold humidity down.
• Keep doors and windows closed unless you are told otherwise. Plastic sheeting may close off the wet area so the dehumidifiers work on a smaller space.
• Mind the power. Each dehumidifier and air mover draws real power. Keep them on their own circuits, never share an extension cord with other appliances, and call if a breaker trips.
• Don't move or turn the fans. Each air mover is aimed at a wet spot on purpose.
• Keep kids and pets clear of cords, hoses and fan intakes.
Before Repairs Start: Your Fort Myers Drying Checklist
Once a wall is closed or new flooring goes down, any water left inside is sealed in. Check these before anything is rebuilt:
• Final readings in every room on the log match the dry standard.
• The slab has been tested before new flooring, and the test meets the flooring maker's limit.
• The inside of every opened wall has been photographed before it is closed.
• Wet insulation has been replaced, not put back.
• Antimicrobial has been applied wherever gray water or sewage touched.
• The house has no musty smell with the doors closed and the AC running.
• You have the final drying log and photos.

Questions About Water Mitigation and Drying in Fort Myers
What is structural drying?
Structural drying is the process of removing moisture from walls, floors, ceilings and cabinets after a leak, using dehumidifiers, air movers and moisture readings until materials are back to normal dry levels.
How do you know the house is dry?
Wet materials are measured against dry materials of the same type in an unaffected part of the house. When they match, drying is finished. Touch and smell are not reliable.
Will I get mold?
Mold can begin within 24 to 48 hours in humid conditions, which is why drying should start the same day. If mold has already grown, bring in a qualified Florida mold remediation company to remove it.
Does antimicrobial spray replace drying?
No. Antimicrobial treats surfaces touched by gray water or sewage; it does not dry anything. Mold is prevented by getting the materials dry.
Why are there holes drilled behind my baseboards?
The small holes let dry air into the wall cavity, so the inside of the wall can dry without cutting out the drywall. They are covered when the baseboards go back on.
Why is the house so warm during drying?
Dehumidifiers release warm, dry air as they pull water out. Keep the AC running; the warm, dry air is what helps materials give up their water.
Should I open the windows to help it dry?
Usually not in Fort Myers. Humid outdoor air slows drying. Keep the house closed and the AC running unless you are told otherwise.
Can I stay home during drying?
Usually yes after a clean-water leak. Expect noise and warm air around the equipment. Rooms affected by sewage or floodwater should stay closed off.
Can new flooring go down as soon as the walls are dry?
Not until the slab is tested. Concrete holds water longer than walls do, and most flooring makers set a moisture limit the slab has to meet before installation.
What if the power is out after a storm?
Drying needs steady power. Until it is back, remove wet carpet pad, baseboards and anything soaked so the rest can start drying, and keep any generator outside, away from doors and windows.
Do I need professional drying if the leak was small?
If the water stayed on a hard surface and was wiped up right away, probably not. If it reached under flooring, into walls or under cabinets, the hidden moisture usually needs commercial equipment and readings.
Why does my house smell musty after a leak was fixed?
A musty smell usually means something is still wet, often the bottom of a wall, the slab under flooring or insulation above a ceiling. Have moisture readings taken before anything is painted or closed up.
