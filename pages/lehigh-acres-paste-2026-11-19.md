Water Damage in Your Lehigh Acres Home Right Now? Do This First
• Shut off the water. On a well, turn off the well pump at its breaker as well as the main valve.
• Cut power at the breaker to wet rooms, and to the well pump if a line is leaking.
• Turn off the AC at the thermostat if it's leaking.
• On septic, stop using water if toilets or tubs are backing up.
• Photograph everything, including the pressure tank and well equipment if they're involved.
• Start drying the same day. Mold can start within 24 to 48 hours in Florida humidity.

My home is on a well. How do I shut the water off?
Turn off the well pump at its breaker and close the main valve near the pressure tank. That stops the pump from pushing more water into a broken line.
My well pressure tank is leaking into the garage. What do I do?
Turn off the pump at its breaker, close the valve near the tank and photograph the leak. Have a well service company repair the tank, and have the garage walls and floor checked for moisture.
My septic is backing up after heavy rain. What now?
Stop using water, keep people and pets away from the overflow and photograph it. Don't have the tank pumped while the ground is still flooded. Call a septic company, and treat anything the backup touched as sewage. See sewage backup cleanup (/sewage-cleanup/).
Street flooding got into my garage. Is that covered?
Water rising from outside is flood damage, covered by a flood policy rather than standard homeowners insurance. Photograph everything before cleanup.
Who do I call about flooded canals or street drainage?
Canal and stormwater drainage in Lehigh Acres is handled by the Lehigh Acres Municipal Services Improvement District (LAMSID).
My AC closet floor is wet. What should I do?
Turn the AC off at the thermostat, soak up standing water and check whether the drain pan is full. Then have the drain line cleared and the closet walls and floor checked for hidden moisture.
I rent my Lehigh Acres home. Who pays for water damage?
The landlord usually handles the building and its repairs, and renters insurance usually covers your belongings. Tell your landlord right away and photograph everything.
How long does drying take in a Lehigh Acres home?
