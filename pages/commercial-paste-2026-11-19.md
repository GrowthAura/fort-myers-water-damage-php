Water Damage at Your Fort Myers Business Right Now? Do This First
• Make it safe. Keep staff and customers away from wet areas, wet outlets and sagging ceilings.
• Stop the water at the fixture, the riser or the building's main valve. If you lease, call the landlord or property manager.
• Cut power to wet areas at the breaker, or have an electrician do it if the panel is wet.
• Sprinklers went off? Call your fire sprinkler contractor and alarm monitoring company; the system has to be restored and checked.
• Move inventory, records, computers and equipment off the floor.
• Photograph and video everything, including damaged stock and equipment.
• Notify your landlord, association and insurer.
• Start drying the same day.

Asbestos Rules Before Tear-Out in Fort Myers Commercial Buildings
Federal asbestos rules, enforced in Florida by the Department of Environmental Protection (DEP), apply to commercial, institutional and public buildings and to apartment buildings with more than four units. Before wet drywall, ceiling tiles or flooring are cut out, the affected areas need an asbestos inspection by an asbestos consultant, no matter how new the building is. If regulated asbestos above set amounts will be disturbed, DEP has to be notified, normally at least 10 working days ahead; for unplanned emergency work, the notice can follow by the next working day. Raise this early, because it can affect how fast removal starts.
Ceiling Tiles and Drop Ceilings in Fort Myers Offices and Stores
Wet acoustic tiles sag and stain and are usually replaced, but the bigger concern is what's above them: insulation, ductwork, wiring and the roof deck or floor above. A wet tile is a sign to find the source above it. See ceiling leak water damage (/ceiling-leak/).
Fire Sprinkler Water Damage in Lee County Buildings
Water from sprinkler piping has usually sat in the lines for years, so it's often dark, oily and foul-smelling, and it's handled as contaminated water, not clean water. Wet ceiling tiles, insulation and carpet it reaches usually come out. The sprinkler contractor restores the system, and your alarm monitoring company and insurer need to know right away.
Storm and Flood Damage to Fort Myers Commercial Property
Many Lee County businesses deal with both roof leaks and rising water after a hurricane. Wind-driven rain through a damaged roof is usually handled under the property policy; rising water and storm surge usually need flood coverage. See flood and storm cleanup (/flood-cleanup/).
Landlords, Tenants and Property Managers in Lee County
Commercial leases usually say who's responsible for the building, the build-out and the contents. As a general rule, the landlord handles the structure and systems, and the tenant handles improvements, contents and inventory. In strip plazas, water from the unit next door or a shared roof adds another party. Everyone should document everything, notify each other quickly and agree on who calls for drying.
Condo and HOA Buildings in Lee County
In a residential condo, Florida Statute 718.111(11) generally puts the building and common elements on the association's policy and the finishes inside each unit on the owner's. Questions About Commercial Water Damage in Fort Myers
Can work be scheduled so we can stay open?
Drying equipment runs around the clock, but removal and setup can often be scheduled to reduce disruption. Ask when you call.
Who's responsible for water damage in a leased commercial space?
It depends on the lease. The landlord usually handles the building, and the tenant usually handles improvements and contents.
How long does commercial drying take?
Clean-water drying usually takes about 3 to 5 days. Larger buildings, contaminated water and flooring removal take longer.
Is business interruption covered for water damage?
Only if your policy includes it and the loss is covered. Keep records of lost days and income in case it applies.
What should a restaurant do after a leak or sewage backup?
Stop service in affected areas, keep staff out of contaminated water, photograph everything and call. Food areas affected by contaminated water must be cleaned and sanitized, and a sewage backup can mean a state inspection before you reopen.
Do we need an asbestos inspection before wet drywall comes out?
In commercial buildings and apartment buildings with more than four units, Florida's asbestos rules call for one before materials are disturbed, whatever the building's age. Ask about it on the first call so it doesn't hold up drying.
What should we do if our fire sprinklers went off?
Keep people clear, have the sprinkler contractor shut down and restore the system, tell your alarm company and insurer, and start extraction. Sprinkler water is treated as contaminated, so soaked porous materials usually come out.
