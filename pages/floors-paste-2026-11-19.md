Key facts about water damaged floors in Fort Myers
• Most Lee County homes are on concrete slabs, so water spreads sideways under flooring instead of draining away.
• Vinyl plank is waterproof on top but not at the seams; water gets under it and stays.
• Laminate's fiberboard core swells at the edges and rarely recovers.
• Older Fort Myers homes often have terrazzo or tile set in a thick mortar bed, which handle clean water better than newer floating floors.
• Flooring is often the biggest single repair after water damage, because it runs continuously from room to room.
Water on Your Fort Myers Floor Right Now? Do This First
• Stop the source and shut off the water at the fixture or the main.
• Cut power at the breaker to rooms where water reaches outlets or appliances.
• Remove standing water with towels or a wet/dry vac. Don't use a household vacuum.
• Lift rugs and move furniture off wet floors; put foil or plastic under furniture legs on damp carpet.
• Photograph seams, grout lines and baseboards before cleanup.
• Start drying the same day.

Why Fort Myers Slab Floors Trap Water
Concrete is porous. When water gets under flooring, the slab soaks it up and releases it slowly. Vinyl plank, laminate and many underlayments act like a lid, so the slab can't breathe and stays wet for weeks. That's why lifting the flooring is often the fastest way to dry the house, even when the planks look fine. Under-slab leaks are a different problem, because the water comes from below. See slab leak water damage (/slab-leak/).
Signs of Water Under Your Floor in a Fort Myers Home
• Vinyl plank or laminate lifting, peaking or separating at the seams
• Dark or damp grout lines that don't dry
• Tile that sounds hollow when tapped, or cracks that appear suddenly
• Wood boards cupping, with the edges higher than the center
• Swollen or stained baseboards
• A musty smell near the floor
• A floor that feels cool or damp in one area, or warm in one spot if a hot water line is leaking
How Water-Damaged Floors Are Dried in Fort Myers Homes
1. Find and stop the source
A plumber, appliance technician or HVAC technician fixes the cause.
2. Extract surface water
See emergency water removal (/water-removal/).
3. Map the moisture
Meters and a thermal camera map moisture across the floor, the slab and the bottom of the walls.
4. Remove what traps water
Vinyl plank, laminate, carpet pad and swollen baseboards are lifted or removed. Click-together planks in good shape are numbered and set aside.
5. Dry the slab and walls
Dehumidifiers, air movers and sometimes floor-drying equipment run with readings logged daily. See structural drying (/drying/).
6. Confirm the slab is dry
New flooring over a wet slab leads to mold, adhesive failure and warped planks, so the slab is tested before anything goes back down.
Tile and grout over a clean-water leak often dry in about 3 to 5 days. Bare concrete under removed flooring can take longer.
Tile and Terrazzo Floors After a Fort Myers Leak
Tile itself doesn't absorb much water, but grout and the thinset or mortar underneath do. If tiles are firmly bonded and the water was clean, they can usually stay while the slab dries through the grout and edges. Hollow, loose or cracked tiles are replaced. Matching discontinued tile can be the hardest part of the repair, so keep spare tiles if you have them.
Many older Fort Myers homes have terrazzo, sometimes hidden under carpet or newer tile, or tile set in a thick mortar bed. Terrazzo is cement-based, so it usually handles clean water well and dries along with the slab. The bigger risk is the carpet, pad or floating floor laid over it.
Is It Water or Heat? Tile Tenting in Fort Myers Homes
Tile that suddenly pops up into a tent shape, often in a sunny room or along a long run with no movement joints, is usually caused by expansion, not by water. A moisture meter on the grout tells the difference: dry grout points to tenting, wet grout points to a leak. Either way, keep off the raised tiles and have them checked.
Vinyl Plank and Laminate After a Fort Myers Leak
Luxury vinyl plank is waterproof on top, but water still gets under it through the seams and edges, and the planks hold it against the slab. Usually the planks in the wet area are lifted, the slab is dried, and click-together planks are reinstalled if undamaged; glued-down or damaged planks are replaced. Laminate has a fiberboard core that swells and rarely survives.
Wood Floors After a Fort Myers Leak
Wood absorbs water and cups. With quick drying, mild cupping can flatten out. Sanding too soon can cause crowning later, so wood floors should be dried and monitored before any refinishing decision. Wood is less common on Fort Myers slabs and reacts fastest to humidity.
Carpet After a Fort Myers Leak
Carpet can often be saved from a clean-water leak if it's extracted and dried within a day or two. The pad usually isn't worth saving. Carpet soaked by gray water, sewage or floodwater is removed.
Floors in Fort Myers and Lee County Condos
Under Florida Statute 718.111(11), floor coverings inside a unit are generally the owner's to insure with an HO-6 policy, not the association's. Many condo associations also have rules on sound-reducing underlayment under tile, vinyl plank or wood on upper floors, so check them before new flooring goes in. Older Floors and Asbestos in Lee County Homes
Some older vinyl tile, sheet vinyl and the black adhesive under them contain asbestos. In an older home, have the old flooring tested before anyone breaks it, scrapes it or sands the adhesive. Left intact, it's far safer than torn up untested.
Insurance and Continuous Flooring in Fort Myers
This is general information, not advice about your policy. When water damages part of a continuous floor, whether the insurer replaces only the wet area or more depends on your policy. Florida Statute 626.9744 says that when replaced items don't match in quality, color or size, an insurer settling on a repair or replacement basis must make reasonable repairs or replacement in adjoining areas. What counts as reasonable is often where floor claims get argued, so photograph the whole floor before anything is removed, keep a spare plank or tile and ask your adjuster how matching is handled.

LVP vs Tile vs Wood in Fort Myers Slab Homes
Most newer Fort Myers and Cape Coral homes have porcelain tile or luxury vinyl plank on the slab, and many older homes have tile or terrazzo throughout. Tile and terrazzo handle a quick clean-water leak best, because the slab can dry through the grout. Vinyl plank is the floor that most often has to be lifted. Wood floors are less common on slabs here and react fastest to humidity.
Questions About Water-Damaged Floors in Fort Myers
Can vinyl plank flooring be saved after water damage?
Sometimes. Click-together planks in good condition can often be lifted, set aside while the slab dries and reinstalled. Glued-down planks or planks with damaged edges are usually replaced.
How do I know if water is under my tile?
Look for dark grout lines that stay damp, tiles that sound hollow when tapped or swollen baseboards. A moisture meter on the grout and slab confirms it.
Can terrazzo floors get water damaged?
Terrazzo itself usually survives a clean-water leak, because it's cement-based like the slab under it. Cracks, loose sections or sewage soaking through are what lead to repairs, and carpet or vinyl plank laid over terrazzo often has to come up.
Should I pull up my flooring myself?
Photograph everything first, and check with your insurer if you plan to file a claim. Removing flooring yourself can also disturb older materials, such as asbestos in some old vinyl tile or adhesive.
Can I put new flooring down right after the leak is fixed?
Not until the slab reads dry. New flooring over a wet slab can trap moisture, grow mold and fail.
Why is my vinyl plank floor lifting?
Water under the planks, high humidity or a wet slab can push planks up at the seams. Find the water source first, then check the slab with a moisture meter.
Will my floor smell if water is still underneath?
Often, yes. A musty smell near the floor, especially by the baseboards, usually means moisture is trapped under the flooring or in the bottom of the walls.
Will insurance replace my whole floor if only one room got wet?
It depends on your policy and on whether the new flooring can reasonably match. Florida's matching law calls for reasonable repairs or replacement in adjoining areas when items don't match, but how far that goes is decided claim by claim. Keep a spare plank or tile and photograph the whole floor.
