Key facts about burst pipes in Fort Myers
• Freezing is rare here. Most bursts come from aging supply lines, washer hoses, water heater connectors, pinhole leaks in copper and old polybutylene pipe.
• Supply-line water is Category 1 (clean) when caught fast, but it can become Category 2 after a day or two.
• Pressurized lines release far more water than drain leaks, so the wet area is usually much bigger than the puddle.
• Insurance often covers the sudden resulting damage, while the pipe repair itself may not be covered.
Pipe Burst in Your Fort Myers Home? Do This Now
• Shut off the water. Use the valve under the sink or behind the toilet for a fixture line. If you can't find it, or the leak is in a wall or ceiling, turn off the main shut-off, usually where the line enters the house, often near the garage.
• Main valve stuck? Shut the water at the meter box near the street. Some meters need a meter key.
• On a private well, turn off the well pump at its breaker too.
• Cut power at the breaker to rooms where water reaches outlets, cords or appliances.
• Drain the pressure by opening a faucet at the lowest point of the house.
• Turn off the water heater (breaker, or gas to pilot) if the water will be off for a while.
• Move belongings out of the water and lift furniture onto blocks or foil.
• Photograph the broken pipe or line, the water, wet walls and floors before cleanup.
• Call a plumber to repair the pipe, and start water removal and drying the same day.

Why Pipes Burst in Fort Myers Homes Without Freezing
• Braided supply lines to toilets, sinks and faucets. The rubber inside ages and the line can split without warning.
• Washing machine hoses, especially original rubber hoses. See appliance leak water damage (/appliance-leak/).
• Water heater connectors and valves in garages, closets and condo utility rooms. See water heater leak cleanup (/water-heater-leak/).
• Ice maker and refrigerator lines, often thin plastic tubing behind the fridge.
• Attic water lines. Many Florida slab homes, especially repiped ones, run water lines through the attic, where a split shows up as a ceiling leak. See ceiling leak water damage (/ceiling-leak/).
• Pinhole leaks in older copper pipe, which can spray inside a wall for days.
• Polybutylene pipe, a gray plastic used in many homes built from roughly 1978 to 1995 and known for sudden failures.
• High water pressure, which strains every joint and line in the house.
• Lines under the slab. A break below the floor is a slab leak. See slab leak water damage (/slab-leak/).
On the few cold nights Lee County gets, the pipes most at risk are exposed outdoor lines, hose bibs and irrigation backflow assemblies, not the pipes inside the house.

Signs of a Hidden Burst Pipe in a Fort Myers Home

How Far Burst Pipe Water Spreads in a Fort Myers Slab Home
Supply lines are under pressure, and a split line can release several gallons a minute until someone shuts the valve. In a Fort Myers slab home, that water:
• Spreads under tile, vinyl plank and baseboards across several rooms
• Soaks the bottom of drywall, which wicks it upward inside the wall
• Gets behind drywall on block walls, into the gap at the furring strips
• Runs under kitchen and bathroom cabinets and swells particle board bases. See water damaged kitchen cabinets (/cabinets/).
• In a condo, travels through the ceiling into the unit below. See condo water damage (/condo-leak/).
The visible puddle is usually only part of the damage. Moisture meters and a thermal camera show the rest.

Does Insurance Cover a Burst Pipe in Fort Myers?
This is general information, not advice about your policy. Usually yes for the water damage a sudden burst causes: wet drywall, flooring, cabinets and belongings. Common limits:
• The pipe itself may not be covered, especially if it failed from age or corrosion.
• Tear-out to reach the pipe is covered under some policies.
• Slow leaks that ran for weeks are often excluded as gradual.
• Some Florida policies cap plumbing-leak damage through a limited water damage endorsement, often at $10,000 per loss.
• Citizens policies limit emergency water mitigation to $3,000 for eligible non-weather losses if you use your own company instead of its free program.
• Vacant homes can have limits after a set number of days unoccupied. See water damage in vacant homes (/vacant-home/).
Photograph everything, keep the plumber's invoice showing what failed and report the claim promptly. Read how insurance handles water damage in Florida (/insurance/).
How to Prevent the Next Burst Pipe in Your Fort Myers Home
• Replace supply lines and hoses on a schedule. Many plumbers suggest replacing braided supply lines every 5 to 10 years, and many manufacturers recommend replacing washer hoses about every five years.
• Check your water pressure. Florida's plumbing code calls for a pressure-reducing valve when static water pressure is above 80 psi. A simple gauge on an outdoor spigot reads it.
• Know your main shut-off and make sure it turns easily.
• Install leak sensors under sinks, behind toilets, at the water heater and washer, or an automatic shut-off valve on the main. Ask your insurer whether it offers a discount for one.
• Replace polybutylene if your home still has it, and have old CPVC in the attic checked for brittle fittings.
• Turn off the main water when you leave for more than a few days. See water damage in vacant homes (/vacant-home/).
Questions About Burst Pipe Water Damage in Fort Myers
What do I do first when a pipe bursts?
Close the fixture valve or the main shut-off, cut power to wet rooms at the breaker, open a low faucet to drain the pressure and photograph everything. Then call a plumber and start drying the same day.
Why do pipes burst in Florida if it doesn't freeze?
Most bursts here come from aging supply lines and hoses, pinholed copper, brittle attic fittings, high water pressure or failing polybutylene, not freezing.
Can drywall be saved after a burst pipe?
Often yes, if the water was clean and drying starts within a day or two. Drywall that stayed wet longer, swelled or grew mold is usually cut out.
How long does it take to dry after a burst pipe?
Most clean-water burst pipe jobs dry in about 3 to 5 days with dehumidifiers and air movers. Wet concrete, hardwood and long leaks take longer.
Does homeowners insurance pay for the plumber?
Policies usually address the resulting water damage. Repairing the pipe itself may not be covered, though some policies pay to open walls or floors to reach it.
Should I turn off the water heater after shutting off the main?
Yes, if the water will be off for a while. Turn off its breaker, or set a gas heater to pilot or vacation.
My pipe burst while I was at work. Is it too late?
No, but act now. Shut off the water, photograph everything and start extraction and drying today. The hours since the break matter more than the amount of water you can see.
Can a burst pipe in the attic damage my ceiling?
Yes. A split attic line soaks insulation and ceiling drywall, which can sag or fall. Turn off power to that room and stay out from under a bulging ceiling. See ceiling leak water damage (/ceiling-leak/).
Should I replace polybutylene pipe?
Most plumbers recommend it, because the pipe and its fittings can fail without warning. Many Florida insurers also ask about polybutylene and may decline or limit coverage for homes that still have it.
