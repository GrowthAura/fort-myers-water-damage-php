Water Under Your Fort Myers Cabinets Right Now? Do This First
• Shut off the water at the valves under the sink, or the main if you can't find the source.
• Empty the cabinet and photograph everything inside before cleanup.
• Soak up standing water and leave the doors open.
• Turn off the dishwasher or disposal at the breaker if they're near the water.
• Photograph the cabinet floor, the toe kick and the flooring nearby.
• Start drying the same day.

Why Water Hides Under Fort Myers Cabinets
Cabinets sit on a base with a closed space between the cabinet floor and the slab. Water that gets under there is trapped with no airflow. It soaks the bottom of the drywall behind the cabinets, the flooring under the toe kick and the cabinet floor itself, and in Southwest Florida humidity that hidden space is a common place for mold to grow.
How Water-Damaged Cabinets Are Dried in Fort Myers Homes
1. Stop the source
A plumber or appliance technician fixes the leak.
2. Empty and open
Cabinets are emptied and the toe kicks removed.
3. Map the moisture
Meters and a thermal camera check the cabinet floors, the wall behind and the flooring underneath.
4. Push dry air in
Small holes are drilled or the toe kick space is opened so dry air can reach under and behind the cabinets.
5. Remove what can't be dried
Swollen particle board panels, and wet drywall behind the cabinets if needed.
6. Dry and repair
Dehumidifiers and air movers run with readings logged daily, then damaged panels, toe kicks and drywall are replaced. See structural drying (/drying/).
Done early, this often saves the cabinets and countertop. When a leak sat for weeks or cabinets are badly swollen, replacement is often the better choice.
Does the Floor Run Under Your Fort Myers Cabinets?
How the flooring meets the cabinets changes the size of the repair. Tile laid before the cabinets went in usually runs under them, so it can stay while the cabinets are dried, but replacing that tile can mean pulling cabinets. Vinyl plank is normally installed up to the cabinets, not under them, so it can usually be lifted without touching the cabinets, though water can still be trapped under the cabinet bases. See water damaged floors (/floors/).
When Do Countertops Have to Come Off in Fort Myers Kitchens?
Replacing a whole run of base cabinets usually means removing the countertop. Granite and quartz can sometimes be removed and reinstalled, but there is a risk of cracking, and laminate countertops usually can't be reused. That's why drying cabinets in place and replacing only the damaged panels is worth trying first.

Bathroom Vanities After a Fort Myers Leak
Vanities have the same weak point as kitchen cabinets: a particle board floor under the sink plumbing. Toilet supply leaks and overflows also run under vanities and into the wall behind them. Toilet overflows can be Category 2 or 3, so a vanity soaked by one is often replaced rather than dried. See sewage backup cleanup (/sewage-cleanup/).

How to Prevent Cabinet Water Damage in Your Fort Myers Home
• Check under sinks once a month for drips and damp spots.
• Replace old braided supply lines and corroded shut-off valves.
• Check under-sink filter and reverse osmosis fittings when you change the filters.
• Use a leak alarm or a waterproof cabinet mat under the kitchen sink.
• Don't store paper goods directly on the cabinet floor, where a slow leak can hide.
• Close the valves under the sinks when you leave home for an extended time. See water damage in vacant homes (/vacant-home/).

Questions About Water-Damaged Cabinets in Fort Myers
Can swollen particle board cabinets be fixed?
Not the swollen part. Particle board doesn't shrink back once it swells. The damaged panel, often just the cabinet floor, can be replaced, or the whole cabinet if it's widely damaged.
How do I know if there's water behind my kitchen cabinets?
Look for swollen toe kicks, a musty smell, peeling edges or damp flooring near the cabinets. A moisture meter or thermal camera confirms it.
Do kitchen cabinets have to be removed after water damage?
Not always. Many cabinets can be dried in place by removing the toe kicks and pushing dry air under and behind them. They're removed when badly swollen, contaminated or hiding mold.
Can I save my granite or quartz countertop if the cabinets are replaced?
Sometimes. Stone countertops can often be removed and reinstalled, but there's a risk of cracking, especially around sink and cooktop cutouts.
Can an under-sink water filter leak into the cabinet?
Yes. Filter housings and reverse osmosis units sit right on the cabinet floor, and a cracked housing or loose tube can drip for weeks. Close the filter's supply valve, dry the cabinet and check the floor panel.
Will new cabinet doors match the rest of my kitchen?
Not always. Older or discontinued styles and finishes are hard to match, which is one more reason to dry and save what you can. If you have a claim, ask your adjuster how matching is handled.
Is a musty smell under the sink a sign of mold?
It can be. A musty smell usually means moisture has been there for a while. Fix the leak, then have the cabinet and the wall behind it checked.
How long does it take to dry cabinets?
Usually about 3 to 5 days for a clean-water leak caught early, longer if water sat under the cabinets for a while.
Can I paint over water stains inside the cabinet?
Not until the cabinet and the wall behind it read dry and the leak is fixed. Painting over damp wood or particle board can trap moisture and hide mold.
