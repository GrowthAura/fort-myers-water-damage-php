Health and safety

Water damage can involve electrical hazards, weakened ceilings, sewage, floodwater and mold. Safety tips on the Site are general. If anyone is in danger, call 911. If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas company or 911 from outside. If someone feels unwell after exposure to sewage, floodwater or mold, contact a doctor.
