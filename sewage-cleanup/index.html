Key facts about sewage backups in Fort Myers

  • Sewage can carry bacteria, viruses and parasites. Keep children, pets, older adults and anyone with a weak immune system away.
  • Stop using all water. Every flush, shower or load of laundry can come back up at the lowest drain.
  • Porous materials touched by sewage are removed, not dried. Tile, concrete and framing are cleaned, disinfected and dried.
  • Many older Lee County homes have cast iron drain lines that crack under the slab and cause repeat backups.

Sewage Backing Up in Your Fort Myers Home Right Now? Do This First

  • Stop using water. No flushing, showers, laundry, dishwasher or sinks.
  • Close it off. Keep people and pets out of the affected area and shut the doors.
  • Cut the power at the breaker if sewage reaches outlets, cords or appliances.
  • Turn off the AC and fans that could move air from the affected area into the rest of the house.
  • Don't vacuum or mop it with household equipment; that spreads the contamination.
  • Photograph everything, then call a plumber to clear the line and a cleanup crew for the house.

Is Sewage Water Dangerous? Staying Safe in a Fort Myers Backup

Yes. Sewage is Category 3, or black water, the most contaminated type of water damage. It can carry bacteria, viruses and parasites, and it soaks into anything porous it touches. Skin contact, splashes and dirty hands are the main risks, which is why cleanup is done in gloves, boots, eye protection and respirators.

Until the area is cleaned:

  • Keep children, pets, older adults, pregnant women and anyone with a weak immune system or breathing problems out of the area.
  • Wash your hands with soap and clean water after touching anything from the affected rooms, and don't eat or drink there.
  • Cover any cuts before you go near it. If a cut or scrape touches sewage and turns red, swollen or painful, see a doctor.
  • Wash clothes that got splashed separately from the rest of the laundry, in hot water.
  • Don't mix bleach with ammonia or other cleaners; the fumes are dangerous. Bleach also can't make soaked carpet, pad or drywall safe again.

Why Sewage Backs Up in Fort Myers Homes

  • Main line clogs. Wipes, grease and roots block the line between the house and the street, so waste comes up at the lowest fixture, usually a shower or tub.
  • Cast iron drains. Many homes built before about 1975 have cast iron drain lines that corrode, scale and crack under the slab.
  • Heavy rain and storm surge. Overloaded sewer systems and flooded ground can push water back into homes during storms.
  • Lift station trouble. Flat Lee County relies on lift stations to pump sewage. When one loses power or fails during a storm, the lowest homes and ground-floor units on that line can back up.
  • Septic problems. Homes on septic can back up when the tank or drain field fails or floods, which is more common in parts of Lehigh Acres and North Fort Myers.
  • Septic-to-sewer conversions. New sewer connections in parts of Lee County, including Cape Coral's utility expansion areas, can expose old drain problems.
  • Toilet overflows. A clogged toilet that overflows with waste is Category 3. A clean-bowl overflow is usually Category 2.

Signs Your Fort Myers Main Sewer Line Is Backing Up

  • Water comes up in the tub or shower when you flush the toilet.
  • Several drains are slow at the same time, not just one sink.
  • Gurgling sounds from drains or the toilet.
  • Sewer smell in bathrooms or near floor drains.
  • Water or waste around the cleanout pipe outside the house.

One slow drain is usually a local clog. Several at once usually points to the main line.

Who Is Responsible for the Sewer Line in Fort Myers?

As a general rule, the lateral line from your house to the public sewer connection is the property owner's responsibility, and the main in the street belongs to the utility. Rules differ between the City of Fort Myers, Lee County Utilities, Cape Coral, Bonita Springs and other providers, so ask your utility if the blockage might be on their side. If several homes on your street are backing up at once, call the utility right away.

Finding your cleanout. The cleanout is a capped pipe, usually 3 or 4 inches across, near the house or near the property line. On many Fort Myers homes it is hidden by landscaping or mulch. Knowing where it is lets a plumber reach the line fast, and sewage around it is a sign the blockage is past your house.

How Sewage Cleanup Works in Fort Myers Homes

  1. 1

    Containment

    Plastic barriers and closed doors keep contamination from spreading to clean rooms. Air filtration may be set up to keep odors and particles inside the work area.

  2. 2

    Protective gear

    Work is done in gloves, boots, eye protection and respirators.

  3. 3

    Extraction

    Sewage and contaminated water are pumped and extracted. See emergency water removal.

  4. 4

    Removal of contaminated porous materials

    Carpet, pad, drywall below the wet line, insulation and swollen particle board vanities are bagged and removed. Drywall is cut in a straight line above the highest wet point, often about 2 or 4 feet up the wall.

  5. 5

    Cleaning and disinfection

    Hard surfaces like tile, concrete and wood framing are cleaned first, then treated with an antimicrobial. Disinfectant works only on a surface that has already been cleaned.

  6. 6

    Drying

    Dehumidifiers and air movers dry the slab and framing, with readings logged daily. See structural drying.

  7. 7

    Rebuild

    Once materials are clean and read dry, drywall, baseboards, vanities and flooring are replaced.

After Cleanup: How to Know Your Fort Myers Home Is Safe Again

Before the rooms go back into use, check that:

  • Every porous material the sewage touched is gone, including the drywall and insulation below the wet line.
  • Hard surfaces have been cleaned and then disinfected, not just sprayed.
  • Moisture readings in the slab and framing are back to normal.
  • There is no sewage smell with the doors closed. A smell that lingers means something contaminated is still in place.
  • A plumber has cleared the line and confirmed it drains, ideally with a camera inspection that shows what caused the backup.
  • If the AC ran while sewage was in the house, the air handler and nearby ducts have been checked.
  • You have photos, the list of removed items and the drying log.

Sewage Backups in Fort Myers Condos and Multi-Unit Buildings

In condo buildings, a blockage in a shared drain stack can push sewage into lower units, often the ground floor. Report it to building management immediately so everyone stops using water. Who pays depends on where the blockage was and your condo documents: a shared line is often the association's responsibility, while a line serving only your unit is often yours.

Sewage After Storms and Flooding in Lee County

After hurricanes and heavy rain, floodwater and sewer overflows often mix. Treat any water that came in from outside, or up through the drains during a storm, as Category 3.

  • Power outages can stop lift stations, so avoid flushing and running water until power is back in your area.
  • A flooded septic drain field can't take water until the ground drains; keep water use to a minimum.
  • Keep children out of standing water in yards and streets after a storm.

Does Insurance Cover Sewage Backup in Florida?

This is general information, not advice about your policy. It depends on the cause and your policy:

  • Sewer or drain backup is often excluded unless your policy has a water backup endorsement. Check your declarations page.
  • A sudden pipe break inside the home is often covered for the resulting damage, even when the pipe itself is not.
  • Cast iron drains that wore out over time are often treated as wear and tear for the pipe, while some resulting damage may still be covered.
  • Flood and storm surge need a separate flood policy.
  • Additional living expenses may apply if a covered loss makes the home unlivable.

How to Prevent Another Sewage Backup in Your Fort Myers Home

  • Don't flush wipes, even ones labeled flushable, and keep grease out of the kitchen sink.
  • Have the main line camera-inspected if you have repeat clogs or an older home with cast iron drains.
  • Ask a plumber whether a backwater valve makes sense for your home.
  • Know where your outdoor cleanout is, and keep it clear of mulch and plants.
  • If you are on septic, keep up with pumping and inspections, and keep cars and heavy equipment off the drain field.

Questions About Sewage Backup Cleanup in Fort Myers

What is sewage backup cleanup?

It is the removal of black water from a home: containment, extraction, removal of contaminated porous materials, cleaning and disinfection of hard surfaces, and drying of the structure.

Can I clean up sewage myself?

A small clean-bowl overflow on tile can be cleaned with gloves and disinfectant. Real sewage on carpet, drywall or more than one room should be handled by a professional crew, because contaminated porous materials must be removed, not wiped.

Is a toilet overflow without waste still sewage?

Not quite. A clean-bowl overflow is usually Category 2. It still needs pad and some drywall removed and an antimicrobial applied, but it is not handled like a sewage backup unless it sat for a day or more.

Can I just use bleach on it?

Bleach can help on hard, already-cleaned surfaces, but it can't make soaked carpet, pad, drywall or insulation safe. Those have to come out. Never mix bleach with ammonia or other cleaners.

Why does sewage come up in my shower when I flush?

The main drain line is usually blocked. Water from the toilet can't get past the blockage, so it rises at the lowest drain, often the shower or tub. Stop using water and call a plumber.

How long does sewage cleanup take?

Removal and disinfection usually take one to two days for a bathroom or one room. Drying the slab and framing takes several more days before rebuilding starts.

Is it safe to stay home after a sewage backup?

Not always, but keep the affected area sealed off and stay out of it. If the only bathroom is affected, or someone in the home is vulnerable, staying elsewhere is safer.

Should I run the AC after a sewage backup?

Keep it off until the affected area is closed off, so contaminated air isn't pulled through the system. If it ran during the backup, have the air handler and nearby ducts checked.

Will the smell go away?

Yes, once contaminated materials are removed and surfaces are disinfected and dried. A smell that lingers usually means something contaminated is still in place.

When can I use the toilet again?

Only after a plumber has cleared the line and confirmed it drains properly.

Who pays for a sewage backup in a condo?

It depends on where the blockage was and your condo documents. A shared line is often the association's responsibility; a line serving only your unit is often yours.

My home is on septic. Is the cleanup different?

The cleanup inside the house is the same: Category 3 rules apply. The difference is the cause. A septic company has to pump and inspect the tank and drain field before you use water again.
