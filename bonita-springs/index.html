Water Damage in Bonita Springs Right Now? Do This First

  • If it's flooding, wait for the all-clear before going back in, and keep the power off.
  • Shut off the water at the fixture or the main if the water is from inside.
  • If river water reached outlets or the panel, leave the power off until an electrician checks it.
  • Measure and photograph the water line on the walls before cleanup; flood adjusters use it to set the scope.
  • Start removing wet materials as soon as the water recedes; mold can begin within 24 to 48 hours.

Imperial River Flooding in Bonita Springs: What Should I Know?

  • The river drains a big area. Its watershed is about 300 square miles and its headwaters are east of I-75, so it can keep rising after the rain in town has stopped.
  • Flooding can happen without a hurricane. Heavy rain over several days can raise the river.
  • Floodwater is contaminated. Drywall, insulation, carpet and particle board it soaks usually come out.
  • Flood insurance is separate. Standard homeowners policies don't cover rising water.
  • Photograph the water line on the walls before cleanup, for the flood claim.
  • New NFIP flood policies usually take 30 days to start, so buy coverage before storm season.

Coastal Condos Near Bonita Beach

Condo buildings near Bonita Beach face both storm surge and the everyday leaks of any multi-unit building: water heaters, AC drains, washers and toilets leaking into units below. Under Florida Statute 718.111(11), the association and owners generally split responsibility depending on what's damaged and what the condo documents say. Report leaks to management right away and photograph everything.

Seasonal Homes in Bonita Springs

Before leaving, shut off the main water and the water heater, set the AC to control humidity, have the AC drain line cleared, arrange regular checks (especially after storms) and install leak alarms. When you return, check the AC, under sinks, the water heater and ceilings, and photograph anything you find with the date.

Permits and the 50% Rule in Bonita Springs
