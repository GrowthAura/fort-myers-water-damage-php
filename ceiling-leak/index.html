Key facts about ceiling leaks in Fort Myers

  • A ceiling stain means the insulation and drywall above are already wet. Painting over it does not fix it.
  • In slab homes with the AC in the attic, most ceiling leaks come from above: the air handler, ducts or the roof.
  • In two-story homes and condos, the usual source is a bathroom, laundry or unit above.
  • Wet drywall loses strength fast; a sag or bubble can come down without warning.

Water Coming Through Your Fort Myers Ceiling? Do This Right Now

  • Get people and pets out from under it. Wet drywall is heavy and can fall without warning.
  • Turn off power at the breaker for any room where water drips from a light fixture, ceiling fan or smoke detector. Don't touch a wet switch.
  • Stop the source if you can. If your AC is in the attic, switch it off at the thermostat. If a bathroom above is leaking, shut off its water. If rain is coming in, focus on catching the water.
  • Catch the water with buckets, towels and plastic sheeting, and move furniture, rugs and electronics out of the way.
  • Don't push on a bulge or stand under it. If a bubble keeps growing and you must relieve it, stand to the side, make one small hole at the lowest point with a long screwdriver and let it drain into a bucket.
  • Photograph and video the ceiling, the drip and anything damaged before you clean up.

Is a Sagging Ceiling in Your Fort Myers Home About to Fall?

A flat stain is a warning; a sag or bubble is an emergency. Drywall is gypsum held together by paper. Once it is soaked it loses strength, and the weight of the water pulls it away from the screws. Treat it as dangerous if you see:

  • A bulge or bubble in the ceiling or the paint.
  • Cracks or a sagging line along a drywall seam.
  • Screw heads popping through the surface.
  • Water dripping from more than one spot.
  • A ceiling that feels soft when gently pressed from a ladder at the edge.

A sagging section almost always has to be cut out and replaced once the area is dry.

Why Is My Fort Myers Ceiling Leaking When It Isn't Raining?

Most often it is the AC: a clogged condensate drain line or sweating ducts in the attic. In two-story homes and condos it can be a bathroom, laundry or kitchen above. Some homes also have water lines running through the attic, where a pinhole leak shows up as a ceiling stain.

Why Ceiling Leaks Are So Common in Fort Myers Homes

  • Attic air handlers. Many Southwest Florida homes have the air handler in the attic, where a clogged condensate line overflows straight onto the ceiling.
  • Humid attics. Hot, humid attic air makes cold ducts sweat and drip through ceiling vents.
  • Afternoon storms and hurricanes. Wind-driven rain gets past flashing, vents and soffits, especially after storms that loosen shingles or tiles.
  • Two-story homes and condos. Bathrooms and laundry rooms above living spaces send leaks straight down.
  • Plumbing in the attic. A supply line in the attic leaks directly onto the ceiling.

Why a Ceiling Stain in a Fort Myers Home Is More Than a Paint Problem

By the time a stain shows, water has already soaked through the insulation above and the drywall itself. In Florida humidity that moisture rarely dries on its own, and left alone it leads to:

  • Mold on the back of the drywall and in the insulation, which can begin within 24 to 48 hours.
  • Stains that bleed back through fresh paint.
  • Sagging and eventual collapse.
  • Rot in ceiling joists or roof decking if the leak keeps going.

Should You Go Into the Attic in a Fort Myers Home?

Only if it is safe. Fort Myers attics get very hot, and a misstep goes straight through the ceiling.

  • Never go above a ceiling that is sagging, soft or dripping.
  • Step only on the joists or a walkboard, never on the drywall between them.
  • Go early in the morning, bring a flashlight and keep someone at the bottom of the ladder.
  • Look for a full AC drain pan, a wet duct, a dripping pipe or daylight through the roof deck. Don't try to fix anything up there.

If you're not sure, leave it to the HVAC company, plumber or roofer, and to the moisture mapping that follows.

How a Wet Ceiling Is Dried or Replaced in Fort Myers

  1. 1

    Find and stop the source

    An HVAC technician, plumber or roofer fixes the cause. A tarp goes on the roof if rain is still getting in.

  2. 2

    Measure the moisture

    A moisture meter and thermal camera show how far the water spread across the ceiling and down the walls, which is usually wider than the stain.

  3. 3

    Decide what stays

    A small, recent, clean-water stain can often be dried in place. A ceiling that sagged, stayed wet for days or has wet insulation above it is usually opened up.

  4. 4

    Remove wet insulation and damaged drywall

    Soaked insulation is bagged out and the wet section of ceiling is cut back to sound drywall.

  5. 5

    Dry the framing and attic space

    Dehumidifiers and air movers run until the joists and remaining drywall read dry. See how structural drying works.

  6. 6

    Repair

    New insulation, new drywall, tape, texture and paint. Knockdown and other textures common in Fort Myers homes are matched to the rest of the ceiling so the patch doesn't show.

Water often runs along joists and down the inside of walls, so the walls below the stain are checked too. If water reached the floor, emergency water removal comes first.

Popcorn Ceilings in Older Fort Myers Homes

Many older homes in Fort Myers and Cape Coral have textured "popcorn" ceilings. Some textures applied before the mid-1980s contain asbestos. Before a wet popcorn ceiling is scraped or cut, have a sample tested, and don't sand or scrape it yourself.

Condo Ceiling Leaks in Fort Myers: Water From the Unit Above

  • Tell building management and the unit owner above right away so the water can be shut off.
  • Photograph your ceiling, walls and floor before anything is removed.
  • Notify the association in writing and call your HO-6 insurer.
  • Expect drying in both units: the source unit's floor and your ceiling are usually wet at the same time.

Does Insurance Cover a Leaking Ceiling in Florida?

This is general information, not advice about your policy. It depends on what caused the leak:

  • Sudden leaks from a burst supply line, an appliance or an AC overflow are often covered for the resulting damage. See burst pipe water damage.
  • Roof leaks after a storm may be covered when wind damaged the roof. Hurricane claims usually carry a separate hurricane deductible.
  • Roof leaks from age or wear are often excluded.
  • Slow leaks that ran for weeks are often treated as gradual damage.
  • Leaks from the unit above in a condo involve your HO-6 policy, the neighbor's policy and the association.

Photograph the ceiling before anything is removed, keep the repair invoices that show the cause and report the date you found the leak truthfully. Read how insurance handles water damage in Florida.

How to Prevent Ceiling Leaks in Your Fort Myers Home

  • Have the AC condensate line cleared at every service and the float switch tested.
  • Have the roof checked after any big storm, even if nothing looks wrong from the ground.
  • Keep gutters, scuppers and flat-roof drains clear before the summer rainy season.
  • In two-story homes and condos, recaulk showers and tubs and replace old washer hoses upstairs.
  • Glance at the ceilings near the air handler and bathrooms once a month for new stains.

Questions About Ceiling Leaks in Fort Myers

What should I do if water is leaking through my ceiling?

Keep people out from under it, turn off power to wet fixtures at the breaker, stop the source if you can, catch the water and photograph everything. Then have the ceiling measured for moisture.

Should I poke a hole in a ceiling bubble?

Only if it keeps growing and you can do it safely: stand to the side, make one small hole at the lowest point and drain it into a bucket. If the ceiling is sagging across a wide area, keep everyone out of the room instead.

Is water dripping from a light fixture dangerous?

Yes. Turn off that circuit at the breaker, don't touch the switch and keep clear. Have an electrician check the fixture and box before you turn the power back on.

What if water dripped from a smoke detector?

Turn off that circuit and have the detector checked or replaced. Don't count on a smoke detector that got wet.

Why does my ceiling leak only when rain comes from one direction?

Wind-driven rain is getting past flashing, a soffit, a vent or a window on that side of the house. Tell the roofer which direction the storm came from; it narrows the search.

Can my tile roof leak if no tiles are broken?

Yes. On a tile roof the underlayment beneath the tiles keeps the water out. When it wears out, rain gets through even with every tile in place.

Can I just paint over a ceiling water stain?

Only after the leak is fixed and the drywall and insulation are confirmed dry with a moisture meter. Paint over wet drywall traps moisture, and the stain usually comes back.

How long does a wet ceiling take to dry?

A small clean-water area often dries in about 3 to 5 days with drying equipment. If insulation or a large section of drywall has to come out, repairs add time after drying.

Who fixes the ceiling after the leak is repaired?

The plumber, roofer or HVAC company fixes the source. Drying the ceiling, removing wet insulation and replacing drywall is water damage restoration work.

Can a ceiling leak cause mold?

Yes. Wet insulation and the back of the drywall can grow mold within 24 to 48 hours in humid conditions, often before anything shows on the painted side.
