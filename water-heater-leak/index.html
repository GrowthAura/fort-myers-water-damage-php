Key facts about water heater leaks in Fort Myers

  • Most tank water heaters last roughly 8 to 12 years; once the tank rusts through at the bottom, it can't be repaired.
  • In Lee County homes, heaters usually sit in the garage, an interior closet, a condo utility closet or occasionally the attic.
  • Water from a heater closet spreads under baseboards, carpet and vinyl plank into nearby rooms.
  • The heater itself usually isn't covered by insurance; sudden resulting damage often is.

Water Heater Leaking in Your Fort Myers Home? Do This First

  • Close the cold-water valve on the pipe going into the top of the heater. If it won't turn or the leak is heavy, shut off the main water to the house. On a well, turn off the pump too.
  • Cut the power or gas. For an electric heater, switch off its double breaker. For gas, turn the control to Off or Pilot. If you smell gas, leave and call the gas company from outside.
  • Stay away from hot water. Water near the tank can be scalding.
  • Keep clear of wet outlets and extension cords on the garage floor.
  • Move boxes, rugs and stored items out of the water.
  • Photograph the tank, its age label, the floor and the walls before cleanup.
  • Start drying the same day.

Where Water Heaters Sit in Lee County Homes, and Where the Water Goes

  • Garage: the most common spot in Lee County single-family homes. Water spreads across the slab, under the door into the house and into the bottom of garage drywall and stored boxes.
  • Interior closet: common in condos, townhomes and some houses. Water soaks the flooring outside the closet, the hallway and the walls on both sides.
  • Attic: less common but the worst case. A leaking attic heater soaks insulation and comes through the ceiling. See ceiling leak water damage.
  • Condo utility closet: a failed heater can leak into the unit below. See condo water damage.
  • Manufactured homes: the heater closet floor often goes soft. See mobile home water damage.

What Florida's Plumbing Code Expects Around a Fort Myers Water Heater

Florida's plumbing code, based on the International Plumbing Code, sets a few rules that matter when a heater leaks:

  • A drain pan under the heater where a leak would damage the building, piped to an approved place to drain.
  • A relief valve discharge pipe that runs full size to a safe spot where you can see it, never capped or plugged.
  • Thermal expansion control, usually an expansion tank, when a check valve or backflow preventer makes the system closed.
  • A permit is often needed to replace a water heater, so ask your plumber to pull it.

A heater in a closet or attic with no pan is one of the most common reasons a small leak turns into a big repair.

Is Water Heater Water in a Fort Myers Home Clean?

Water straight from the supply line is Category 1, clean water. Water that sits on a garage floor, soaks through dirty carpet or runs for days can become Category 2, which means more cleaning and more removal.

Warning Signs Before a Fort Myers Water Heater Fails

  • Rust-colored hot water or rust at the tank's base
  • Rumbling or popping sounds as sediment builds up
  • Moisture or small puddles around the base
  • A tank older than about 10 years; the serial number on the label usually shows the year it was made
  • Corroded fittings on the pipes at the top
  • A relief valve that drips or a discharge pipe that's wet

On private wells in Lehigh Acres and parts of the north Cape, hard water builds sediment faster, so tanks there often need flushing more often. If you see these signs, have a plumber look at the heater before it fails, and make sure a drain pan is installed.

How to Lower the Risk of a Water Heater Flood in Your Fort Myers Home

  • Install a drain pan under the heater, piped to a drain where possible.
  • Add a leak alarm or an automatic shut-off valve.
  • Flush the tank as the manufacturer recommends.
  • Know where the cold-water shut-off is and make sure it turns.
  • Keep the area around a heat pump water heater's condensate line clear, and check that it drains.
  • Plan replacement before the tank reaches the end of its life.
  • Seasonal homes: turn off the heater and the main water when you leave. See water damage in vacant homes.

Does Insurance Cover Water Heater Leak Damage in Fort Myers?

This is general information, not advice about your policy.

  • Resulting damage to floors, walls and belongings is often covered when the failure was sudden.
  • The water heater itself usually isn't covered, because tanks wear out.
  • Long-running leaks may be excluded as continuous seepage.
  • Some Florida policies cap non-weather water losses, often at $10,000 per loss, through a limited water damage endorsement.
  • Condos: damage reaching other units can involve the association and other owners' policies.

Keep photos, the heater's age label and the plumber's invoice. Read how insurance handles water damage in Florida.

Questions About Water Heater Leaks in Fort Myers

What do I do first when my water heater is leaking?

Close the cold-water valve at the top of the heater, then cut its power at the breaker or set a gas heater to Off. If the leak is heavy or the valve won't turn, shut off the main water.

Is a leaking water heater an emergency?

Yes, if water is spreading or the tank is leaking from the bottom. A leaking tank can fail completely without warning.

Can a leaking water heater be repaired?

A leaking fitting, drain valve or relief valve can often be repaired. A tank leaking from the bottom because of corrosion usually needs replacement.

Can I still use hot water if the heater is leaking?

No. Shut off the supply and the power or gas until a plumber has checked it.

Does my water heater need a drain pan in Florida?

Florida's plumbing code calls for a pan where a leak would damage the building, such as in a closet, an attic or a condo. Ask your plumber to add one if yours doesn't have it.

Why is my heat pump water heater leaking?

Heat pump water heaters make condensate like an AC. A clogged or loose condensate line is a common cause; leaks from the tank or fittings are handled like any other tank.

My water heater leaked into the garage. Do I need to worry about the walls?

Yes. Water on a garage slab often wicks into the bottom of the drywall and can run under the door into the house. Have the walls and nearby rooms checked with a moisture meter.

How long does it take to dry after a water heater leak?

Most clean-water leaks dry in about 3 to 5 days. Leaks that soaked under vinyl plank, reached several rooms or ran for days take longer.

How long do water heaters last in Florida?

Most tank heaters last roughly 8 to 12 years. Have one that's 10 years or older checked for rust and leaks at the base.
